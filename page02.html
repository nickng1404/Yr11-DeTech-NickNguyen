What is NZQA Level 1 for Year 11 like?

You need a total of at least 80 credits from all courses in a year. You are required to earn 20 credits in Literacy and Numeracy to complete Level 1 for Year 11.

Help students understand how to earn credits from tests throughout the year and final exams to obtain a certificate.

What is NZQA Level 2,3 for Year 12,13 like?

At level 2, you'll have the same requirements as at Level 1: 60 credits across all subjects and 20 mandatory credits in Literacy and Numeracy. However, at Level 2, the important thing is that if you want to graduate, go to university, or get a job, you need to achieve NZQA Level 3, or possibly Level 2.
